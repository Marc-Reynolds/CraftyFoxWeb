Formatting support

Help preparing the layout of an existing manuscript for print or ebook. Discuss the format you need and the work involved before getting started.

For self-publishing

KDP publishing support

Guidance with preparing your book for Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing and understanding the submission steps. You stay involved in the publishing decisions.

How the work is approached: AI tools can assist the work. Marc reviews the deliverables. The scope and approach are agreed with you before work begins.

A conversation, then a plan

How it works

  1. 01

    Share your idea

    Tell Marc what you want to write, who it is for and where you are feeling stuck. Early notes and half-formed ideas are a starting point.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope

    Talk through the help you need and agree what the work will cover before moving ahead.

  3. 03

    Develop the outline together

    Work through the shape of the book with Marc, organising your ideas into an outline that supports your next step: writing.
